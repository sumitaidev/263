APAAR ID Creation Roadmap

APAAR (Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry) is a unique ID under NEP 2020. It acts as a lifelong academic passport, tracking your progress from school to higher education. Follow this roadmap to understand the process.

1. Contact Your Institution

Approach your school, college, or university. They are the authorized bodies to initiate the APAAR ID generation process.

2. Submit Required Documents

Provide your Aadhaar card, enrollment number, date of birth, and a passport-sized photograph.

3. Parental Consent

Since APAAR is a permanent ID, it requires explicit consent from your parent or legal guardian via the UIDAI/DigiLocker system.

4. ID Generation

The institution uploads your data to the national portal. A unique 12-digit APAAR ID is generated instantly.

5. Link with DigiLocker

Once generated, link your APAAR ID with DigiLocker to automatically fetch and verify all your academic documents.
